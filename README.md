## 🛑 The Old Way is Broken

Most resume checkers give you a generic "score" and tell you to "make your bullets pop." I wanted something that actually moved the needle. 

Trajekt was built around one brutal question:
> *"If I applied to this exact role today, would I actually survive the ATS screen?"*

Instead of just checking for typos, Trajekt acts like a ruthless technical recruiter. It cross-references your projects, technical skills, and impact metrics against real-world Job Descriptions to tell you **exactly what you are missing** and **what to fix next.**

---

## ⚡ What Trajekt Actually Does

*   **📄 Deep Extraction:** We don't just read text; we parse your technical stack, project depth, internship exposure, and GitHub/LinkedIn signals.
*   **🎯 Brutally Honest Readiness Scores:** See how you stack up against FAANG, Product, or Core Engineering benchmarks.
*   **🔍 Precision JD Matching:** Paste a Job Description. Trajekt will highlight the explicit keywords and signals you lack.
*   **🗺️ The 4-Week Execution Plan:** Stop "learning more backend" aimlessly. Get a week-by-week, actionable roadmap tailored to patch your specific skill gaps.

## 🔐 Anonymous-First Architecture

Your resume is your data. 
*   **No forced sign-ups:** You can run a full analysis without ever creating an account.
*   **Memory-Only Parsing:** If you don't sign in, your PDF is parsed in memory and the data is wiped the second your session ends.
*   **Zero Data Selling:** We don't sell your info to third parties. Ever.

*(Read our full [Privacy Policy](https://trajekt.in/privacy) and [Terms of Service](https://trajekt.in/terms)).*

## 🛠️ The Tech Stack

**The Face (Frontend)**
*   **Framework:** Next.js (App Router) + React + TypeScript
*   **Styling:** Tailwind CSS (Custom Minimal Brutalism)
*   **Motion:** Framer Motion

**The Brains (Backend)**
*   **Engine:** FastAPI + Python
*   **Database:** PostgreSQL + SQLAlchemy + Prisma
*   **AI/Vector:** ChromaDB + RAG + LLM-powered analysis
*   **Auth:** Auth.js (NextAuth) + Google OAuth

**The Muscle (Infrastructure)**
*   **Hosting:** Vercel (Frontend) + Railway (Backend & DB)
