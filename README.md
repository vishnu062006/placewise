## Why I built Trajekt


Most resume checkers give you a score and leave you wondering what to do next.


I wanted something more useful.


Trajekt was built around a simple question:


> **"If I applied to this role today, how prepared would my profile actually be?"**


Instead of only checking formatting or ATS keywords, Trajekt looks at your technical skills, projects, internships, education, role-specific requirements, and job descriptions to give you a clearer picture of your placement readiness.


The goal isn't to tell you that your resume is "good."


The goal is to tell you **what to fix next.**


---


## What you can do with Trajekt


### 📄 Analyze your resume


Upload your resume and get a structured analysis of your:


- Technical skills
- Projects
- Internships
- Education
- Certifications
- GitHub & LinkedIn signals
- Technical profile


### 🎯 See your placement readiness


Get a role-specific readiness score based on your current profile and the expectations for your target role.


### 🔍 Match against a Job Description


Have a specific internship or job in mind?


Paste the JD and Trajekt compares it against your resume to identify:


- Matching skills
- Missing requirements
- Role-specific gaps
- Areas that need improvement


### 🧩 Find your skill gaps


Know exactly what's holding your profile back instead of randomly learning technologies.


### 🗺️ Get a personalized roadmap


Trajekt turns your gaps into an actionable improvement plan.


Instead of:


> "Learn more backend."


You get a structured roadmap based on your actual profile and target role.
